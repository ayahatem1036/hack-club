Common cat types

Did you know that there's about 75 standardised cat breed?

Cats are recognised as a beloved pet around the world :3

Here is a list of some common cat breeds!!!

    Maine Coon

Maine Coon cats are known as "gentle giants" for their loud and social dog-like personality

    Ragdolls

Ragdolls are known to be lazy but they are easy to befriend as they are social. They got their name from their tendency to just plop down like ragdolls

    British Shorthair

British Shorthair cats are known for their round face. These cats are known to grow slowly
